# Billing FAQ

## Invoice cadence

Enterprise plans are invoiced monthly in arrears. Invoices are sent on
the 5th business day of the following month and are due net-30.

## Currency and tax

All invoices are denominated in USD. VAT and local sales taxes are
itemised separately on the invoice for EU and APAC customers.

## Late payment

Accounts become delinquent after 45 days past due. A 1.5% monthly
late fee applies; service is throttled at 60 days and suspended at 90.
