# Vendor Onboarding

## Initial intake

New vendors must complete the security questionnaire and submit a
SOC 2 Type II report (or equivalent) before any contract is signed.
The legal team reviews master service agreements; procurement reviews
order forms.

## Approval thresholds

- Up to $10,000/year: department head approval.
- $10,000 to $100,000/year: VP-level approval plus security review.
- Above $100,000/year: CFO approval and a full risk assessment.

Vendor records are maintained in the vendor management system; do
not pay invoices for vendors that are not yet onboarded.
