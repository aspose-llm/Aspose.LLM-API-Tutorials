# Refund Policy

Enterprise subscribers may request a refund within 30 days of the
invoice date, provided that the service has not been substantially
used. "Substantial use" is defined as more than 20% of the billed
seat-hours consumed during the billing period.

Refunds are processed within 14 business days of approval. All refund
requests must be submitted through the customer-success team; billing
does not accept direct requests from customers.

Self-service tier and trial accounts are not eligible for refunds.
