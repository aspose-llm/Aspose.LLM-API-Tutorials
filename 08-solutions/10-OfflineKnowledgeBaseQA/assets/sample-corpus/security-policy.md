# Security Policy

## VPN access

VPN access requests must be approved by the requesting employee's
direct manager and the security team. Approvals expire after 12
months and require renewal.

## Password requirements

Passwords for production systems must be at least 16 characters and
include letters, digits, and a symbol. SSO is mandatory for all SaaS
tools that support it; password-only accounts are blocked at the
identity provider.

## Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@example.com
or the `#sec-incidents` channel within 1 hour of discovery.
