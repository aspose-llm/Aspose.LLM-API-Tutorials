# Customer Success Runbook

## Refund handling

Customer-success owners receive refund requests through the support
portal. After verifying eligibility against the refund policy, route
the approved request to billing through the `#cs-billing-requests`
Slack channel; do not email billing directly.

## Escalations

P1 customer escalations must be acknowledged within 30 minutes during
business hours. Page the on-call CS lead in PagerDuty for any account
representing more than $50,000 in annual contract value.
