SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Software Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
1 May 2026 between Halyard Systems Ltd. ("Provider") and Northbeam
Industries B.V. ("Client").

Section 1.1 Services. Provider shall furnish the development,
hosting, and support services described in Schedule A.

Section 1.2 Fees. Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in
Schedule B within thirty (30) days of invoice receipt.

Section 4.3 Term and Renewal. The initial term of this Agreement is
twenty-four (24) months commencing on the Effective Date. This
Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twenty-four
(24) month terms unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least one hundred and twenty (120) days prior to the
end of the then-current term.

Section 6.2 Exclusivity. During the term of this Agreement and for
a period of twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Client shall not
engage any third party to develop, host, or support software
solutions that compete with or substantially duplicate the
functionality of the Services delivered hereunder.

Section 8.2 Indemnification. Provider shall indemnify, defend, and
hold harmless Client and its affiliates, directors, officers,
employees, and agents from and against any and all damages, losses,
liabilities, judgments, costs, and expenses (including reasonable
attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to Provider's
performance of the Services, without cap or limitation of any kind.

Section 9.4 Intellectual Property Assignment. Client hereby assigns
to Provider all right, title, and interest, including all
copyrights, patents, and trade secrets, in and to any and all
inventions, improvements, derivative works, and feedback conceived,
developed, or contributed by Client or its personnel during the
course of this engagement, whether or not arising from the use of
the Services.

Section 11.1 Confidentiality. Each party shall maintain the
confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information for a
period of ten (10) years following termination or expiration of
this Agreement.

Section 14.2 Non-Compete. For a period of thirty-six (36) months
following termination of this Agreement, Client shall not, directly
or indirectly, solicit, hire, or engage any employee or contractor
of Provider who was involved in delivering the Services, anywhere in
the European Union.

Section 17.4 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of the British Virgin Islands,
without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

Section 18.1 Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising out of or
relating to this Agreement shall be finally resolved by binding
arbitration administered in Tortola, British Virgin Islands, in
English, with each party bearing its own costs and attorneys' fees
regardless of outcome.

Section 20.5 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, including all
schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and
supersedes all prior negotiations.
